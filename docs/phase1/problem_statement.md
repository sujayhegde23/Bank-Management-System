# Problem Statement Analysis

## 1. Problem Statement

Banks handle a large amount of customer, account, and transaction data every day.
Managing these operations manually or through disconnected systems can lead to
delays, data inconsistency, difficulty in tracking transactions, and security issues.

The Bank Management System is proposed as a centralized software application that
helps manage customers, bank accounts, and financial transactions efficiently.

The system will allow authorized users to perform common banking operations such as
creating accounts, depositing money, withdrawing money, transferring funds, viewing
account details, and checking transaction history.

The system will also provide administrative features for managing customers,
employees, accounts, and transaction records.

---

## 2. Objectives

The main objectives of the Bank Management System are:

- To automate common banking operations.
- To maintain customer and account information in a centralized system.
- To allow secure deposit, withdrawal, and fund transfer operations.
- To maintain a complete record of financial transactions.
- To provide customers with easy access to account details and transaction history.
- To allow bank employees to manage customers and bank accounts.
- To restrict access to banking features based on user roles.
- To improve the accuracy, security, and efficiency of banking operations.

---

## 3. Existing Problems

Traditional or manually managed banking systems may face several problems:

- Customer records may be difficult to manage and retrieve.
- Manual processing may increase the chance of human errors.
- Transaction tracking may become difficult.
- Updating account information may take more time.
- Duplicate or inconsistent records may occur.
- Unauthorized users may gain access if proper access control is not implemented.
- Generating reports and viewing transaction history may be difficult.

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

The proposed Bank Management System will provide a centralized platform for managing
banking activities.

Customers will be able to access their account information and perform permitted
banking operations.

Bank employees will be able to manage customers, accounts, deposits, withdrawals,
and other banking operations.

Administrators will be able to manage system users and monitor banking records.

The system will maintain transaction records and provide authentication and
role-based access control to protect sensitive banking information.

---

## 5. Project Scope

The Bank Management System will include the following major features:

- User authentication
- Customer management
- Bank account creation and management
- Balance enquiry
- Deposit
- Withdrawal
- Fund transfer
- Transaction history
- Customer profile management
- Account activation and deactivation
- Employee management
- Basic banking reports

---

## 6. Out of Scope

The following features will not be included in the current academic prototype:

- Real banking network integration
- UPI integration
- Credit card processing
- Loan processing
- Stock or investment services
- Real payment gateway integration
- Inter-bank settlement
- Integration with RBI or other external banking institutions
